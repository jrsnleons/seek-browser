**Install:** download `Seek-1.3.apk` below on the tablet and open it. It updates 1.2 in place. From now on, Seek can update itself.

- **Updates from inside the app.** Once a day Seek asks GitHub whether there is a newer release. When there is, it tells you, and "Update to Seek …" appears at the top of the menu: download, and the tablet's installer opens. Turn the daily check off, or check now, under Settings › Updates.
- **Tab icons as letters**, like Search: Settings › Look › Tab icons.
- **Two menus instead of one crowded one.** The ••• button is for the page in front (find, reading, text size, split, hide, share). The new ☰ button is for the app (new tab, all tabs, bookmarks, history, downloads, settings).
