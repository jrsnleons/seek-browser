**Install:** download `Seek-1.2.apk` below on the tablet and open it. It updates 1.1 in place.

**Split screen, up to three pages.** Hold a tab, drag it onto the page and let go: Seek asks whether to split the screen or open it there. Hold a tab without moving it for the tab menu.

**Faster:** ads hidden earlier in the load, background tabs paused and put to sleep after 30 idle minutes, a background budget set from the tablet's memory, addresses looked up while you type, a tab list that updates in place, and site icons remembered between launches.

**Looks:** subtle motion, a start page with your most-visited sites, and an adaptive launcher icon.

**New in Settings:** Lite mode (no autoplay video, no web fonts), tint the frame to match each site, hide the top bar while scrolling, compact density, and the Inter typeface.
