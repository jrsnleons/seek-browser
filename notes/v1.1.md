**Install:** download `Seek-1.1.apk` below on the tablet and open it.

- Tab switcher, tabs across the top as an option, swipe a tab sideways to close it
- Split view: two pages side by side
- Floating video (picture-in-picture), private tabs, per-site text size
- Pure-black AMOLED theme, new-tab backgrounds, darken bright sites
- Two-finger back and forward, pull down to reload
- Optional search suggestions and EasyList blocking; bookmark import and export
